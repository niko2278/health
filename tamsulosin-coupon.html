How to save on Tamsulosin

  1. Get one of the free cards above on your phone or print it.
  2. When you drop off or pick up your Tamsulosin prescription, give the card numbers to the pharmacist.
  3. Ask the pharmacist to compare the card price with your insurance copay, and pay the lower one.
  4. If the price is still high, check another pharmacy nearby. The same card can give different prices.

Tamsulosin questions

Can I use a discount card for Tamsulosin?

Yes. You can show a free discount card at most U.S. pharmacies when you fill Tamsulosin. The pharmacist applies the discounted price if it is available for your drug and dose.

How much does Tamsulosin cost without insurance?

It depends on the strength, the quantity and the pharmacy. Prices for the same prescription can differ a lot between stores, so check the price with a card before you pay.

Can I use a Tamsulosin coupon with my insurance?

Not on the same prescription. You can choose the discount card price instead of your insurance when it is lower. Payments made with a card may not count toward your deductible.
